## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are admins at Korean call operator companies. They use the product during working hours to create, test, compare, and manage synthetic voices for production call flows and operator-facing voice experiences.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel trustworthy, operational, and precise. It should read like a professional control surface rather than a creative toy, with calm confidence and clear status communication.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light operational theme with slate, mineral, and teal tones instead of a generic startup gradient palette. The experience should feel structured and inspectable, with a strong sense of system status, audio workflow discipline, and admin reliability.

### Design Principles
1. Surface system state clearly with concise labels, health context, and predictable hierarchy.
2. Keep the interface calm and low-noise, but not bland; use accent color sparingly for intent and action.
3. Optimize for repeated daily work such as testing voices, reviewing outputs, and managing assets.
4. Prefer layouts that make controls and results easy to compare side by side.
5. Treat every empty, loading, and error state as operational guidance rather than decorative filler.
